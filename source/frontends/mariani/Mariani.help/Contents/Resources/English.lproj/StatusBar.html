The Status Bar

The status bar appears at the bottom of the main window, and provides quick access to some common functions.

Quick Controls

On the left edge of the status bar:

  1. Reboot the emulator.
  2. Control+Reset

Drives

Circle buttons represent individual floppy and hard disk drives configured for the emulator. The circles can take one of the following appearances:

  1. Drive is empty.
  2. Drive contains a disk image.
  3. Drive contains a write-protected disk image.

You can also click on the circles to insert, create, examine, or eject disk images.
